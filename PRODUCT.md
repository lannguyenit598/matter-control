# MatterControl product

MatterControl is the operational control plane for complex legal Matters, initially transactions
and corporate legal work. Legal teams need to understand state, blockers, evidence and the next
permitted action without confusing execution with verified truth.

Core surfaces are My Work, Matter overview/Control Room, work grid, documents and exact versions,
proof review, filing cycles, planning, agenda, history and status brief. Local extensions add
private Saved Views, workspace-published playbooks, Matter intake and versioned guidance.
Focus is personal execution support; it never changes legal state. Quick Search returns only
currently authorized records.

Roles are Workspace OWNER/MEMBER and explicit Matter OWNER/EDITOR/VIEWER; personas do not grant
permissions. Workspace ownership alone does not reveal Matter contents. See the
[permission matrix](docs/requirements/permission-matrix.md), [golden workflows](docs/product/three-golden-workflows.md),
[glossary](GLOSSARY.md) and [design direction](DESIGN.md).

Keep EN/VI interfaces, self-hosted Be Vietnam Pro, truthful counts, accessible controls and
recoverable commands. AI, connectors and reminders are not implied by the local feature set.
The capability baseline is not a claim that every item has passed browser, real-provider or
production acceptance.
