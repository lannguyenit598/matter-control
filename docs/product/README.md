# Approved product baseline

Source: Product Owner's [Step 00 request](../ai-sdlc/step-00-request.md).

- Name: **MatterControl**.
- Positioning: **Control Plane for High-Stakes Legal Work**.
- Core positioning: MatterControl gives legal and transaction teams a single operational view of matter state, dependencies, blockers, evidence, decisions, and change history.
- Product principle: **The System of Operational Truth for Complex Legal Matters.**
- Initial vertical: **Transactions & Corporate Legal Work**.
- Product Discovery is closed.

The subsequent [Step 00A request](../ai-sdlc/step-00a-request.md) approves product direction, [P0/P1/P2 capabilities](../requirements/p0-p1-p2-priorities.md) and [three Golden Workflows](three-golden-workflows.md). [Canonical Product Context](canonical-product-context.md) is a partial source baseline: original Documents A/B are confirmed missing. Detailed transitions, access rules, retention, acceptance thresholds and workloads remain Step 01 inputs/questions. No implementation backlog or final architecture is implied.
