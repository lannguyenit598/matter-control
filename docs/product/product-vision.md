# Product Vision and Outcomes

Owner: Codex. Status: APPROVED_BY_PO (2026-10-24; subject 572835a; DEC-016); identity and direction already approved.

Make the current operational truth of complex legal matters understandable and actionable: what is true, what is missing, what blocks progress, who can act next, and what changed. Reduce uncertainty without pretending the software makes legal judgments.

Principles: preserve legal domain semantics; separate execution/proof/dependency/time/attention; explain derived states; retain evidence/version lineage; make permissions effective everywhere; preserve accountable history; make dense work efficient; report deterministically from explicit scope and time.

| Outcome | Measure / method | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Manager comprehension | Approximately 10 seconds to identify major matter state; test protocol in AC-137 | User-supplied usability target, NOT_PROVEN |
| Operational trust | All approved critical state/authorization/concurrency scenarios pass with reproducible evidence | Proposed release criterion, no runtime proof |
| Full operational coverage | All 20 P0 contracts and GW-01/02/03 pass; exceptions explicitly reported | Scope and AC protocol approved; runtime NOT_RUN |
| Bulk productivity | 50–200-row tasks can be completed with keyboard/inline/bulk/import/export; record time/errors and compare against baseline | User-supplied workflow scale; improvement threshold not invented |
| Report confidence | Every reported change resolves to an authorized source/event and stated baseline/as-of | Proposed RULE-016/019 |

No SLA, legal compliance guarantee, quantified ROI or cost feasibility is asserted. Use [NFRs](../requirements/srs.md) and OBD-09/10/11 to validate workloads, thresholds and recovery needs before release.
